Planning at a glance

Good to know
before you book.

Straight answers to the questions couples ask most. Final policies and pricing are confirmed with the venue team.

01

Venue access

Venue access is 10 AM–10 PM. Grounds-only access is generally noon–10 PM. One extra Friday or Saturday hour may be available for $500.

02

Reserve a date

Hold a date for seven days with a $500 refundable deposit. A $2,500 down payment books the date.

03

Food

Cooked food and sit-down meals require an approved preferred caterer. The kitchen is for preparation, not cooking.

04

Planning support

Two planning meetings are included: one after booking and one about 30 days before the event.

05

Final payment

The remaining balance is due 60 days before the event. Bank transfer, card, and cash are accepted; cards add a 3% fee.

06

End of the night

Guests and vendors have one hour after the event to remove personal items and complete cleanup tasks.
