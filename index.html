Inside the barn

Ready for the whole day

Host support, farmhouse and round tables, chairs, bistro tables, custom bar, preparation kitchen, four restrooms, bridal suite, groom’s lounge, fireplace, loft, grand staircase, dance floor, Wi-Fi, climate control, sound, televisions, and wireless mics.

Across the grounds

Room to spread out

Four ceremony and activity settings—the bridge, gazebo, arbor, and timber pavilion—plus paved private parking, mountain views, patio seating, a gas fire pit, playground, yard games, exterior lighting, and portrait locations.

Good to know

Simple planning facts

Up to 175 seated or 300 open-house guests. Venue access is generally 10 AM–10 PM. Dates may be held seven days with a $500 refundable deposit; booking begins with $2,500 down. Tours are by appointment and the team typically responds within 24–48 hours.
